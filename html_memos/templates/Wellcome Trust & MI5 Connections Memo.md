## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This memo documents the factual, publicly recorded connections between the Wellcome Trust — one of the world's largest private medical research funders — and the British domestic intelligence service, MI5. These connections are not speculative; they are a matter of public record. They are, however, rarely discussed together in a single analytical frame. The central figure bridging these two worlds is **Baroness Eliza Manningham-Buller**, former Director General of MI5 and subsequent Chair of the Wellcome Trust Board of Governors. A secondary layer of significance is the role of **Sir Andrew Parker**, MI5 Director General in January 2020, whose service activated MI5's pandemic emergency plan in February 2020 — concurrent with Wellcome Trust's mobilisation of its COVID-19 response under Director **Jeremy Farrar**.

**NEW FINDING:** This memo also documents Farrar's repeated appearances at the Munich Security Conference alongside the **Nuclear Threat Initiative (NTI)**, a biosecurity-focused organization with deep ties to intelligence and defense communities. Most significantly, **in February 2020** — at the exact moment Farrar was organizing the *Proximal Origin* paper — he was physically present at the Munich Security Conference where NTI was simultaneously conducting a tabletop exercise on responding to high-consequence biological threats.

## SECTION 1: KEY INDIVIDUALS

### 1.1 Baroness Eliza Manningham-Buller

Eliza Manningham-Buller is the single most important figure connecting MI5 and the Wellcome Trust. Her career trajectory is as follows:

- Joined MI5 and rose through the ranks over several decades
- Appointed **Director General of MI5 in October 2002**, the most senior position in British domestic intelligence ([MI5 Official](https://www.mi5.gov.uk/about-us/director-general/former-directors-general/eliza-manningham-buller-baroness-manningham), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliza_Manningham-Buller))
- Led MI5 through the aftermath of the **11 September 2001 attacks** and the **7 July 2005 London bombings**, overseeing a major expansion of MI5's counter-terrorism capabilities ([Aviva Investors](https://www.avivainvestors.com/fr-fr/about/external-people/baroness-manningham-buller/))
- Retired from MI5 in **April 2007** ([MI5 Official](https://www.mi5.gov.uk/about-us/director-general/former-directors-general/eliza-manningham-buller-baroness-manningham))
- Subsequently appointed **Chair of the Wellcome Trust Board of Governors** in 2015, becoming the **first woman ever** to hold that position ([Wellcome Trust](https://wellcome.org/press-release/eliza-manningham-buller-be-next-chair-wellcome-trust))
- Her appointment followed an open, formal recruitment process — it was not hidden or informal

### 1.2 Jeremy Farrar

Jeremy Farrar served as **Director of the Wellcome Trust from 2013 to 2023**. During this period, Manningham-Buller served as Chair of the Board — meaning she was, institutionally, Farrar's direct superior on the governing board. Farrar went on to play a central role in the global COVID-19 response:

- Was a **key organiser and participant** in the 1 February 2020 teleconference that led to the drafting of the *Proximal Origin* paper — the paper that shaped the dominant "natural origin" narrative around COVID-19 ([US Congress](https://www.congress.gov/event/118th-congress/house-event/116185/text), [Science Magazine](https://www.science.org/content/article/politicians-scientists-spar-over-alleged-nih-cover-up-using-covid-19-origin-paper))
- The *Proximal Origin* paper acknowledges **Wellcome Trust funding for author Andrew Rambaut** ([Nature Medicine](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7095063/)), but did not initially disclose Farrar's significant behind-the-scenes involvement in its drafting and publication
- A US House of Representatives Select Subcommittee memo (March 2023) found that **Farrar was more involved in drafting Proximal Origin than previously known**, making at least one direct edit to the paper on 17 February 2020, and possibly should have been credited as an author ([House Oversight PDF](https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2023.03.05-SSCP-Memo-Re.-New-Evidence.Proximal-Origin.pdf))
- Served as an adviser to **SAGE** (the UK's Scientific Advisory Group for Emergencies) during the pandemic
- Subsequently appointed **Chief Scientist at the World Health Organization (WHO)**
- **Regular participant** at the Munich Security Conference, appearing on biosecurity panels

### 1.3 Sir Andrew Parker (Lord Parker of Minsmere)

Sir Andrew Parker was **Director General of MI5 from April 2013 to April 2020** ([MI5 Official](https://www.mi5.gov.uk/about-us/director-general/former-directors-general/sir-andrew-parker), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Parker,_Baron_Parker_of_Minsmere)) — meaning he was the serving head of MI5 at the precise moment COVID-19 emerged as a global crisis in January–February 2020. Key facts:

- Parker confirmed that **MI5 activated its pandemic emergency plan in early February 2020**, after receiving intelligence that COVID-19 would significantly impact the UK — weeks before most public health bodies had publicly acknowledged the severity of the threat ([The Times](https://www.thetimes.com/uk/healthcare/article/sir-andrew-parker-mi5-chief-acted-on-coronavirus-pandemic-warning-in-early-february-nmg2l55pb))
- He gave a BBC interview as he prepared to step down in April 2020, explicitly discussing **coronavirus and MI5's adaptation** to the pandemic
- Parker's tenure as MI5 DG (2013–2020) **directly overlapped** with Farrar's tenure as Wellcome Trust Director (2013–2023)
- Parker's departure in April 2020 was **planned and expected** — he had completed a standard seven-year term, and his successor Ken McCallum was announced in advance on 30 March 2020 ([UK Parliament](https://committees.parliament.uk/writtenevidence/22707/html/))

## SECTION 2: THE NTI CONNECTION — MUNICH SECURITY CONFERENCE & BIOSECURITY NETWORK

### 2.1 What is the Nuclear Threat Initiative (NTI)?

The Nuclear Threat Initiative is a Washington, D.C.-based nonprofit founded in 2001 by former US Senator Sam Nunn and philanthropist Ted Turner. While originally focused on nuclear security, NTI has expanded significantly into **biological threat reduction and biosecurity governance**. NTI operates at the intersection of:

- National security and intelligence communities
- Global health policy
- Pandemic preparedness and biosecurity
- Public-private partnerships with entities like the World Economic Forum and the Munich Security Conference

### 2.2 Jeremy Farrar's Presence at Munich Security Conference with NTI

Jeremy Farrar has been a **regular participant** at the Munich Security Conference (MSC), one of the world's premier forums for defense and security policy, often appearing on biosecurity panels.

**Most significantly:**

- **15 February 2020**: Farrar appeared at MSC 2020, speaking at the "Townhall on the Coronavirus Outbreak" — at the exact moment the real COVID-19 pandemic was unfolding globally
- **February 2020**: At the same conference, NTI convened senior leaders from around the world for a **scenario-based tabletop exercise** on responding to high-consequence biological threats ([NTI Official](https://www.nti.org/events/preventing-global-catastrophic-biological-risks/), [Global Biodefense](https://globalbiodefense.com/event/preventing-global-catastrophic-biological-risks-launch-event-for-ntis-2020-munich-security-conference-tabletop-exercise-report/))
- This tabletop exercise was designed to test global readiness for deliberate or accidental biological events
- The exercise took place **while Farrar was simultaneously organizing the publication of the *Proximal Origin* paper** (1 February teleconference, published late February 2020)

### 2.3 The Timing: February 2020

February 2020 represents a remarkable convergence of events:

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1 Feb 2020 | Farrar organizes teleconference leading to *Proximal Origin* paper |
| Early Feb 2020 | MI5 activates pandemic emergency plan under Director General Andrew Parker |
| 15 Feb 2020 | Farrar speaks at Munich Security Conference "Townhall on Coronavirus Outbreak" |
| Feb 2020 | NTI conducts biosecurity tabletop exercise at Munich Security Conference |
| 17 Feb 2020 | Farrar makes direct edit to *Proximal Origin* manuscript |
| Late Feb 2020 | *Proximal Origin* paper published in Nature Medicine |
| April 2020 | Parker completes planned seven-year term and retires from MI5 |

### 2.4 NTI's Pattern of Biosecurity Exercises at Munich Security Conference

This was not an isolated event. NTI has conducted multiple biosecurity tabletop exercises at the Munich Security Conference:

- **2019**: NTI conducted a tabletop exercise at MSC focused on responding to deliberate biological events ([Georgetown GHSS](https://ghss.georgetown.edu/dbe_response/ttx/))
- **February 2020**: The exercise documented in this memo
- **March 2021**: NTI partnered again with MSC to conduct another tabletop exercise — this time simulating a fictional **monkeypox outbreak** to test global readiness ([NTI Paper PDF](https://www.nti.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/NTI_Paper_BIO-TTX_Final.pdf))

This establishes a **pattern** of NTI using MSC as a venue for biosecurity scenario planning — and Farrar's presence in this network.

## SECTION 4: ANALYSIS AND SIGNIFICANCE

### 4.1 The Connection is Institutional and Open
These are not secret or hidden connections. The link between MI5 and the Wellcome Trust runs directly through Manningham-Buller's public career trajectory. What is notable is that this connection is **rarely foregrounded** in discussions about Wellcome Trust's role in COVID-19 policy, despite being entirely a matter of public record.

### 4.2 A Remarkably Small Network
What this research reveals is that a **very small number of individuals** sat at the intersection of:
- British domestic intelligence (MI5)
- Global health philanthropy (Wellcome Trust)
- Pandemic policy and narrative formation (COVID-19 response, *Proximal Origin*, SAGE, WHO)
- Biosecurity governance networks (NTI, Munich Security Conference)

The same institution (Wellcome Trust) that was chaired by a former MI5 Director General was also the institution whose Director played a central, and initially undisclosed, role in shaping the dominant scientific narrative about COVID-19's origins — while simultaneously participating in biosecurity exercises run by an organization (NTI) with deep ties to the intelligence and defense communities.

### 4.3 MI5's Early Pandemic Awareness
The confirmation that MI5 activated its pandemic emergency plan in **early February 2020** — earlier than most public health bodies — raises legitimate questions about information flows between the intelligence community and health policy bodies during this critical period. Whether any such information sharing occurred with Wellcome Trust leadership has not been publicly documented.

### 4.4 Farrar's Undisclosed Role in Proximal Origin
The US House Subcommittee finding that Farrar was more involved in *Proximal Origin* than disclosed — making direct edits while the paper acknowledged Wellcome Trust funding for one of its authors (Andrew Rambaut) — adds a further layer of significance to understanding the institutional context in which that paper was produced. Farrar's role as both:
- Director of the funding organization (Wellcome Trust)
- Organizer of the 1 February 2020 teleconference
- Direct editor of the manuscript

...was not fully disclosed at publication, raising questions about editorial transparency.

### 4.5 The Munich Security Conference as a Convergence Point
The Munich Security Conference emerges as a key venue where intelligence, defense, biosecurity, and global health communities converge. Farrar's presence there in February 2020 — speaking on coronavirus while NTI ran a biosecurity exercise, and while he was simultaneously organizing *Proximal Origin* — represents a remarkable convergence of roles and timing.

- The connections documented here are **factual and sourced**, but the significance one draws from them depends on analytical judgement
- Intelligence community engagements are by nature often undocumented in public sources
- This memo is based entirely on **open source, publicly available information**
- NTI's tabletop exercises are openly documented and not secret activities
- Parker's April 2020 departure was planned and expected — he completed a standard seven-year term
