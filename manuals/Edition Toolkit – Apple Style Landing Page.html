Core Tools

Everything you need to publish.

Four focused utilities that each do one thing exceptionally well, designed to work seamlessly together.

📥
Extract

medium_to_md

Extracts any Medium article into a clean Markdown file, preserving embedded images, formatting, and structure — ready for further processing.

🔧
Validate

md_harden

Normalizes and validates Markdown files, generating a review document with suggested edits so you can approve changes before committing them.

📄
Convert

md_to_pdf

Converts hardened Markdown into professionally typeset PDFs. Supports two rendering engines — Typst (default) and WeasyPrint — for full flexibility.

📚
Publish

md_to_booklet

Converts Markdown articles into page-imposed, print-ready booklets using Pandoc and XeLaTeX. Perfect for physical or digital distribution.

Workflow

From draft to print in four steps.

A clean, repeatable pipeline that takes your content from raw Medium post to polished publication.

1

Extract from Medium

Point the toolkit at any Medium article URL. It downloads the content and converts it to a structured Markdown file, with images saved locally alongside it.

medium_to_md --url https://medium.com/your-article
2

Harden & Review

Run the hardener to normalize formatting, fix inconsistencies, and generate a review document. Inspect the suggested changes and apply the ones you approve.

md_harden --input article.md --review
3

Convert to PDF

Transform your hardened Markdown into a beautifully typeset PDF. Choose your rendering engine and apply a custom style preset to match your brand.

md_to_pdf --input article.md --engine typst --style elegant
4

Generate a Booklet

Produce a print-ready booklet with proper page imposition, ready for physical printing or digital distribution as a zine or pamphlet.

md_to_booklet --input article.md --output booklet.pdf

Rendering Engines

Two engines. One great output.

Choose the rendering engine that fits your workflow — or switch between them effortlessly.

Default

Typst

A modern, blazing-fast typesetting system designed for precision and beauty. Typst is the default engine in Edition Toolkit, offering clean output with minimal configuration and excellent support for custom styles.

Alternative

WeasyPrint

A powerful HTML/CSS-based PDF renderer that gives you full control using web standards. Ideal for teams already comfortable with CSS-driven layouts and wanting pixel-perfect web-to-print output.
